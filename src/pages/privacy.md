Bubble Pop is a simple bubble-popping game. This policy explains what
information the app collects and why.

## What we collect

- **A device ID** — a random identifier generated on your device the first
  time you play. It is not derived from any hardware identifier or
  advertising ID, and cannot be used to identify you personally. It's how
  the server recognizes repeat visits from the same install: leaderboard
  submission rate limits, and your Orb balance/unlocks, are all keyed to it.
- **A nickname** — the name you type in before submitting a score. Choose
  anything you like; don't enter your real name if you'd rather not share it.
- **Your score and the level you reached**, when you submit to the
  leaderboard. Relax mode never sends anything; Competitive mode only sends
  this when you explicitly tap "Submit Score."
- **An opaque player ID from Google Play Games or Steam.** On Android, this
  happens only if you choose to sign in (Settings or the pause menu, never
  required). On Steam, this happens automatically the first time you launch
  the game — no button press or prompt required — using your Steam identity.
  Either way, this lets your Orb balance, unlocks, and scores follow you
  across every device signed into the same account. We only ever receive one
  opaque ID (your Google Play Games player ID or your SteamID64) — never your
  name, email, or profile photo.
- **Purchase records**, if you buy Orbs: which product you bought, how many
  Orbs it granted, and your device ID. Payment itself (card details, etc.)
  is handled entirely by Google Play Billing or Steam — we never see or
  store it. A Steam purchase separately passes your SteamID64 to our server
  to start the transaction with Valve; that use is ephemeral and not stored
  beyond completing the purchase. (Signing in via Steam, described above,
  does store your SteamID64 as your account identifier — the same way a
  linked Google Play Games ID is stored.)

## What we don't collect

- No name, email address, or profile data of any kind, even when you sign in.
- No location data.
- No contacts, photos, or other device data.
- No advertising or analytics SDKs are included in the app.
- No payment/card details — that's handled entirely by Google Play Billing
  or Steam, never by us.
- No data is sold or shared with third parties, beyond what's needed to
  process a purchase you initiated (Google/Valve, as your payment provider).

## How the data is used

Submitted nicknames, scores, and levels are shown publicly on the in-app
leaderboard (daily, weekly, and all-time views) to any player. The device ID
(and, if you've signed in, the linked Google Play Games player ID) is used
server-side to prevent rapid repeat leaderboard submissions, and to track
your Orb balance, unlocks, and purchase history so they're there the next
time you play.

## Data retention

Leaderboard entries, Orb balances, unlocks, and purchase records are
retained to keep the app working the way you'd expect across sessions and
devices. If you'd like your data removed, contact us using the details
below with your nickname and approximate time of play — we'll need enough
to find the right device ID or linked account.

## Children's privacy

Bubble Pop does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
The only inputs beyond gameplay itself are a self-chosen, freeform
nickname, and an optional sign-in that only ever exchanges an opaque
player ID, never a name or email.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
